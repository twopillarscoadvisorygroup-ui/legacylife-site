The monthly bookkeeping checklist: 8 steps to close your books

By the Legacy Life team · November 16, 2026 · 6 min read

Keeping your books current doesn’t have to be overwhelming. The secret is a short, repeatable routine at the end of each month, often called “closing the books.” Here’s a checklist you can follow.

The checklist

  1. Record all income. Make sure every sale and deposit is entered and matched to the right customer or category.
  2. Record all expenses. Enter bills, receipts and card charges. Snap photos of paper receipts so nothing gets lost.
  3. Reconcile every account. Match your books to your bank and credit card statements line by line. This catches errors, missing items and even fraud.
  4. Review unpaid invoices. Check who owes you money and follow up on anything past due.
  5. Review your unpaid bills. Make sure you know what’s coming due so there are no surprises.
  6. Check payroll. Confirm payroll and related payments are recorded correctly.
  7. Run your reports. Look at your profit and loss statement, balance sheet and cash flow.
  8. Ask “does this look right?” Compare to last month and the same month last year. Big jumps deserve a closer look.

Tip: set a recurring calendar reminder for the same day each month, like the 10th. Consistency matters more than perfection.

Habits that make it easier

  • Separate accounts. Use a business bank account and card only for business.
  • Connect your bank feeds to your bookkeeping software so transactions come in automatically.
  • Categorize weekly. Ten minutes a week beats three hours at month‑end.
  • Keep notes. Jot down what unusual transactions were for while you still remember.

Why it’s worth it

Current books let you spot cash‑flow problems early, price your work with confidence, apply for credit quickly and hand clean records to your tax professional. It’s one of the simplest ways to take stress out of running a business.
