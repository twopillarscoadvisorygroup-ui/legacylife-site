Sole proprietor, LLC or corporation? Choosing a business structure

By the Legacy Life team · November 16, 2026 · 7 min read

Choosing how to set up your business is one of the first big decisions you’ll make, and it’s one you can change later as you grow. Here’s a simple look at the most common options.

Sole proprietorship

If you start doing business on your own without forming an entity, you’re usually a sole proprietor by default.

  • Pros: simplest and cheapest to start, with little paperwork.
  • Cons: no legal separation between you and the business. Business debts and lawsuits can reach your personal assets. It can also be harder to build business credit.

Limited liability company (LLC)

An LLC is formed by filing with your state.

  • Pros: helps separate business liabilities from your personal assets when you follow the rules, flexible management and generally lighter formalities than a corporation.
  • Cons: state filing fees and ongoing requirements, such as annual reports in many states.

Corporation

A corporation is also formed with the state and is owned by shareholders.

  • Pros: strong liability separation, easier to bring in investors and issue ownership shares.
  • Cons: more formalities, such as bylaws, a board, meetings and minutes.

What about “S‑Corp”? An S corporation isn’t a separate type of entity you form with the state. It’s a tax election that an eligible LLC or corporation can make with the IRS. Whether it makes sense depends on your income and situation, so talk it through with your tax professional.

Questions to ask yourself

  • How much personal risk does my work involve?
  • Do I plan to bring on partners or investors?
  • Will I need business loans or credit soon?
  • Can I keep up with the ongoing filings each structure requires?

Whatever you choose, keep it clean

An entity only protects you if you treat it as separate: its own EIN, its own bank account, signed agreements and filings kept current. Mixing personal and business money is one of the fastest ways to weaken that protection.
