CARF Accreditation Preparation

Get survey‑ready with documentation built to the standards.

CARF accreditation opens doors to payers, referral partners and state contracts. But the paperwork can overwhelm a small team. CARF requires written policies and procedures for nearly every part of your operation, and they must be approved, shared with staff and reviewed regularly.

Legacy Life Inc. prepares that documentation for you. We work from the current CARF standards manual for your program area and map every policy to the standard it meets, so your team and the surveyors can find it fast.

Why Legacy Life Inc.

Our founder, Charese Adesalu, brings more than 30 years of accounting, finance and compliance experience, including work as a Compliance Officer and Healthcare Operations Consultant.

We understand both the clinical‑program side and the financial and governance standards that many programs overlook.

Who we help

  • Behavioral health and substance use treatment programs
  • Residential, outpatient, intensive outpatient and partial hospitalization programs
  • Recovery housing, reentry and community services organizations
  • New programs preparing for their first survey, and accredited programs preparing for re‑survey

What we prepare

  • Gap analysis against the CARF standards for your programs
  • Policy and procedure manual, organized by standard
  • Required written plans: strategic, risk management, accessibility, technology, health and safety, cultural competency, and performance improvement
  • Corporate compliance program and code of conduct
  • Financial planning and management documentation
  • Personnel, training and credentialing records structure
  • Outcomes and performance measurement framework
  • Mock survey and readiness report
  • Quality Improvement Plan (QIP) after your survey

How it works

  1. Free 30‑minute consultation

    We learn about your programs, locations and target survey date.

  2. Gap analysis

    We review what you have and give you a prioritized list of what is missing.

  3. Document development

    We write or revise your policies and plans, then review drafts with you.

  4. Readiness review

    We conduct a mock survey and help your staff prepare for interviews.

  5. After the survey

    We help you write your Quality Improvement Plan and keep documents current.

Plan ahead. CARF recommends starting preparation about 12 months before your desired survey date.

Written Information Security Plans (WISP) for Tax Professionals

Protect your clients, your PTIN and your practice.

Every paid tax preparer needs a written information security plan. Under the Gramm‑Leach‑Bliley Act and the FTC Safeguards Rule, tax and accounting professionals are considered financial institutions, regardless of size. When you renew your PTIN, you certify that you understand your data security obligations.

Your WISP must describe the safeguards you actually use: your software, your devices, your staff and your vendors. Legacy Life Inc. builds that plan for you.
