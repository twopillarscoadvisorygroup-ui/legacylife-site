About Legacy Life

We help owners build businesses that outlast them.

Legacy Life Inc. is a financial consulting and business compliance firm based in Baltimore, Maryland, serving business owners across the country.

Why we exist

Good businesses shouldn’t fail over paperwork.

Too many hard‑working owners lose sleep, money and opportunities because their books are behind, their credit isn’t ready or a filing slipped through the cracks.

We started Legacy Life to change that. We bring order to the money side and the paperwork side of business, and we explain everything in plain language along the way.

Because when your business is organized, funded and in good standing, it can do more than pay the bills. It can become a legacy.
