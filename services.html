Our services

Everything your business needs to stay organized and in good standing.

We focus on two things: the money side and the paperwork side of running a business. Pick one service or let us handle all three.

Financial consulting

Bookkeeping & cash flow

Know your numbers without becoming an accountant. We keep your books clean and current and help you plan ahead.

  • Monthly bookkeeping and account reconciliation
  • Simple monthly reports in plain English
  • Cash‑flow forecasting for slow and busy seasons
  • Budgeting and pricing check‑ups
  • Separating personal and business spending
  • Catch‑up bookkeeping if you’ve fallen behind

Great for: owners who are busy running the business and want a clear picture of where they stand each month.

Financial consulting

Business credit & funding readiness

Lenders look for specific things. We help you build them, so when you’re ready to grow, you’re ready to apply.

  • Business credit building plan
  • Personal credit review and improvement steps
  • Funding‑readiness review before you apply
  • Organized financial statements for lenders
  • Debt and payment planning
  • Help understanding loan and credit options

Great for: owners planning to apply for a loan, line of credit or equipment financing in the next year.

Business compliance

Entity setup & compliance

Start on the right foot and stay in good standing. We track the deadlines so you don’t have to.

  • Choosing and forming the right entity (LLC, corporation and more)
  • EIN and business bank account setup
  • State annual reports and renewals
  • Business license and permit tracking
  • Registered agent and good‑standing checks
  • Organized records and compliance calendar

Great for: new businesses getting started and established ones that want peace of mind about filings.

A note on taxes: Legacy Life does not prepare or file tax returns. We keep your records organized and tax‑ready and are happy to work alongside your tax professional.
